# 15. Risks

## Technical Risks
- Scaling the social feed under heavy load may increase latency.
- Eventual consistency may create temporary inconsistencies in notifications.
- Third-party integrations can become reliability bottlenecks.

## Business Risks
- Feature overload may reduce product clarity and adoption.
- Underestimating onboarding friction can harm engagement.
- High infrastructure costs may erode profitability at lower volumes.

## Mitigations
- Introduce performance monitoring and load testing early.
- Keep the initial scope focused on core training usage.
- Use managed services and cost dashboards to control spend.
