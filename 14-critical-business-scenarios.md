# 14. Critical Business Scenarios

## Scenario 1: Workout Logging During Peak Usage
A user logs a training session while many other users are active. The system must retain the record and update progress without service degradation.

## Scenario 2: Coach Monitoring Client Progress
A coach needs to view recent training activity for multiple clients. The system must deliver dashboard data quickly and accurately.

## Scenario 3: Social Engagement Spike
A popular post triggers a burst of likes and comments. The platform must remain responsive and preserve the timeline ordering.

## Scenario 4: Recovery After Partial Failure
A service outage or database issue should not cause permanent data loss; the system must recover and resume normal operation.
