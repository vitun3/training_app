# 16. Roadmap

## Phase 1 - MVP
- User registration and authentication.
- Training plans and workout logging.
- Basic progress dashboard.

## Phase 2 - Social Expansion
- Follow relationships and social posts.
- Notifications and reminders.
- Coach-client visibility improvements.

## Phase 3 - Growth and Personalization
- Advanced analytics and recommendations.
- Subscription and challenge features.
- Multi-platform mobile support.
