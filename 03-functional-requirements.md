# 3. Functional Requirements

## Core Capabilities
1. User registration and authentication.
2. Create, edit, and delete training plans.
3. Track workout sessions and log progress.
4. Follow other users and interact through social posts.
5. Receive notifications for training reminders and social activity.
6. Manage profile information, goals, and preferences.

## Business Rules
- A user can belong to multiple training groups or coach-client relationships.
- A workout session must be linked to an existing plan or be created ad hoc.
- Social posts must be associated with a user and optionally with a workout or plan.
- Notifications should be sent only for relevant actions and respect user preferences.
