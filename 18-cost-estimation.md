# 18. Cost Estimation

## Assumptions
- 10k monthly active users in the first year.
- 100k workout records and 50k social events per month.
- Typical cloud deployment with managed PostgreSQL, Redis, object storage, and container orchestration.

## Estimated Monthly Costs
- Compute and containers: $1,500-$3,000
- Managed database: $400-$1,000
- Caching and queueing: $150-$500
- Storage and networking: $200-$700
- Monitoring and support: $300-$800

## Total Estimated Range
$2,550-$6,000 per month depending on traffic and retention.
