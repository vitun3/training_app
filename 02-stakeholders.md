# 2. Stakeholders

## Stakeholder Map
- Product Owner: defines the product vision, prioritizes features, and owns business outcomes.
- End Users: athletes, beginners, and fitness coaches who use the platform daily.
- Development Team: implements and maintains the application.
- DevOps / Platform Team: ensures deployment reliability, observability, and scalability.
- Security Team: defines compliance and secure development practices.
- Executive Sponsors: fund the initiative and monitor strategic alignment.

## Stakeholder Concerns
- Users want a simple, motivating, and reliable experience.
- Coaches need clear visibility into client progress and training plan adherence.
- Engineering needs maintainable services and predictable delivery.
- Business needs measurable adoption and growth with controlled operating costs.
