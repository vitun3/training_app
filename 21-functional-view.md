# 10. Functional View

## Functional Decomposition
- Authentication Service
- Training Plan Service
- Workout Tracking Service
- Social Service
- Notification Service
- Analytics Service

## Responsibilities
- Authentication manages identity and access.
- Training Plan Service handles workout configuration and lifecycle.
- Workout Tracking Service records training sessions and metrics.
- Social Service supports follows, posts, and feed generation.
- Notification Service dispatches reminders and activity alerts.

## Interaction Model
The API layer coordinates these services based on user actions such as plan creation, workout logging, and social engagement.
