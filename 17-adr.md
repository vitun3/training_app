# 17. Architecture Decision Record

## ADR-001: Use Cloud-Native Containerized Services
- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-07-29
- Context: The platform requires fast iteration, strong reliability, and future scalability.
- Decision: Deploy the application as containerized services on a managed Kubernetes platform with managed databases.
- Consequences: Increased operational maturity is required, but the solution is easier to scale and evolve.
